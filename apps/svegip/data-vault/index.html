Who is the client and what decision or outcome do they need from SVE?

Scope

Set the working boundaries. These can be refined after matter creation.

03

Known Information

Separate what is known from what still needs validation.

04

Readiness & Create Matter

Review the intake. Missing non-critical information does not block matter creation; it becomes a tracked open item.

Create Matter will open the project workspace and carry this intake into the matter. Evidence, risks, tasks, documents and open questions are then managed from the project.
